Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Prodbystickie Clip Poster ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner with their own TikTok account.

What the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open ID and display name), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Draft uploads, to send finished videos to the owner's TikTok drafts.
  • The owner's public video list, to find the link of a video after the owner posts it.

How it is stored

TikTok access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app collects no data about other TikTok users.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and delete the stored token file.
