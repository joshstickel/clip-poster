Prodbystickie Clip Poster

Prodbystickie Clip Poster is a personal tool that edits short gaming and streaming clips and sends them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The account owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app, adds the caption and chooses whether to post it.

The tool is used only by its owner for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.
